{{ labels.glossary_title }}
Rank
Position by urgency — escalation probability multiplied by exposure and confidence. Row 1 is the borrower to open first.
{{ labels.column_band }}
Act now is a 70% or higher chance of crossing a covenant threshold at the worst horizon; Amber is 40% or higher; Watch is everything below, listed but not escalated.
{{ labels.column_dated_risk }}
The probability at the worst covenant and horizon, with the date the projection crosses the threshold. Where confidence sits below the floor, the reason is shown instead of a number.
30 / 60 / 90
The same probability at each forecast horizon, so a rising line is visible without opening the case file. A dash means the horizon was suppressed for low confidence.
{{ labels.column_sma_band }}
Special Mention Account classification carried from the borrower's account conduct, independent of the covenant forecast.
{{ labels.column_what_changed }}
What moved since the previous completed run, naming the prior band and the new one. A dash means this borrower has not yet been compared with a prior run.
